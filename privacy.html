🔒 Your Privacy Matters

This policy explains how Yarlagadda App collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using our app, you agree to this policy.

📋 Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, email (only when you voluntarily provide)
  • Grievance details, photos, location (with your explicit permission)
  • Device information (model, OS version) for crash analytics
  • App usage data to improve performance

🔧 How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver citizen services (grievance redressal, appointments, welfare schemes)
  • To enable AI‑powered assistance (via Google Gemini API)
  • To ensure security and prevent misuse
  • To communicate updates or respond to your queries

🤖 Google Gemini AI & Third‑Party Services

We may send anonymised text prompts to Gemini API for generating suggestions or auto‑replies. No personal identifiers are shared. Other third parties: Firebase (authentication, database), Google Maps (location picker).

🔐 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers and your device. We use encryption in transit and at rest. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.
