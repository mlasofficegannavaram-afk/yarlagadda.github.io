4. How We Use Your Information

  1. Create and manage your account.
  2. Verify eligibility for government welfare schemes and civic services.
  3. Route grievances to the correct official and track resolution.
  4. Match blood donors with emergency requests.
  5. Match job seekers with relevant opportunities.
  6. Send notifications about your account, grievances, and announcements.
  7. Display your profile and public interactions to other users — only the fields you choose to make public.
  8. Republish the MLA Office's own Facebook posts as News Articles and Development Activities inside the App.
  9. Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate registrations.
  10. Comply with legal obligations.

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data.
  • Use your Aadhaar number for any purpose other than scheme eligibility verification.
  • Share your contact information with advertisers.
  • Use your data for targeted advertising.
  • Import private Facebook messages, third-party profiles, or news screenshots from other publishers.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

6.1 Where data is stored

  • Supabase servers: Data centers in the Mumbai region (India) where available; otherwise Singapore.
  • Your device: Local encrypted storage for offline access.

6.2 Security measures

  • Encryption in transit: All communication uses HTTPS/TLS 1.2+.
  • Encryption at rest: Sensitive fields (Aadhaar) are encrypted at rest.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Database policies restrict access on a need-to-know basis.
  • Role-based access: Only authorised constituency staff can view grievance data or contact details in imported posts.

6.3 Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of a valid deletion request.
  • After 24 months of inactivity, we anonymise or delete your account data, except where retention is required by law.
  • Grievance records: retained for 5 years as required by government audit rules (anonymised after account deletion).
  • Imported Facebook posts: retained for the lifetime of the source Facebook post. Removed on request within 30 days.
  • Crash logs and analytics: retained for 90 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view it in the App under Profile.
  • Correct your data — edit it in the App under Profile.
  • Delete your account and data — see the dedicated page at yarlagadda.best/delete-account.html or email us at mlasofficegannavaram@gmail.com. We will process deletion within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Object to imported content — if you appear in an imported Facebook post and want it removed, email us.
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your account or revoking permissions in device settings.
  • Lodge a complaint — with our Grievance Officer or with the Data Protection Board of India.

11. AI-Generated Content

The App uses Google Gemini to generate summaries, reels, chat responses, and form auto-fill suggestions. AI-generated content is labelled with an "AI-generated" badge. The AI does not make decisions that affect your legal rights. Only the specific inputs you submit to AI features are sent to Gemini; we do not send your Aadhaar number, password, or authentication token to any AI service. Imported Facebook posts may be processed by AI to categorize them as News Articles or Development Activities — no authentication data or private messages are ever sent to any AI service.
