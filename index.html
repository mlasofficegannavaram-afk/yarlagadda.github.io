📱 Application Documentation

Yarlagadda is a next‑gen constituency management and citizen services Android application built for transparent governance, grievance redressal, appointment scheduling, welfare schemes, TTD darshan requests, AI‑powered assistance, and real‑time development monitoring.

✨ Core Features

  • Public Grievance Management with real‑time tracking
  • Citizen Welfare & Service Delivery Dashboard
  • Appointment & Visitor Management System
  • Google Gemini AI Integration (smart summarisation & chat)
  • TTD Darshan Request & Festival Calendar
  • Development Activity Monitoring (geo‑tagged)
  • Election & Survey Management (offline‑first)
  • Secure Authentication (Google Sign-In + encrypted Supabase storage)
  • Role-based access: Public · Staff · Technical Admin · MLA Dashboard

🛠️ Technology Stack

  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Row-Level Security)
  • Google Sign-In via Credential Manager API
  • Google Gemini AI SDK (gemini-2.5-flash)
  • Google Maps & Places APIs
  • Room Database + SQLite (offline-first)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (stability monitoring)
  • WorkManager (background sync)
  • MVVM + Repository pattern
  • Encrypted local storage for sensitive fields

✅ Google API Compliance

This website serves as the official public documentation for OAuth consent screen verification, Gemini API usage disclosure, and compliance with Google's limited‑use policy.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. The Yarlagadda App collects only the data needed to deliver citizen services — and never sells your information.

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, mobile number, profile photo
  • Government ID: Aadhaar number (optional — for welfare scheme eligibility only)
  • Location: village, mandal, district, GPS (with your permission)
  • Content you submit: grievances, photos, voice notes, survey responses
  • Contacts: only when you explicitly use the Invite feature
  • Permissions: Camera · Microphone · Location · Contacts · Notifications · Phone number (prefill only)

Read our full privacy policy for complete details on data retention, third-party sharing, encryption, and your rights.

🗑️ Delete Your Account

You can delete your Yarlagadda App account and all associated personal data at any time — either from inside the app or by emailing us.

How to request deletion

  • In-app (fastest): Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • By email: Send a request from your registered email to mlasofficegannavaram@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Account credentials (name, email, mobile)
  • Profile information and Aadhaar number (including encrypted backups)
  • Location history, blood donation profile, job seeker profile
  • Push notification token
  • Comments and reactions (anonymised, disassociated from you)

What is retained (and why)

  • Grievance records — 5 years (government audit requirement, anonymised)
  • Financial records — 7 years (Indian tax law, if applicable)

Timeline: Acknowledged within 7 days · Deleted within 30 days · Backups purged within 30 days of deletion.

🛡️ Data Safety & Compliance

The Yarlagadda App follows Google Play's Data Safety requirements and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Data Safety Summary

  • Encrypted in transit: Yes (TLS 1.2+)
  • Encrypted at rest: Yes (Aadhaar and sensitive fields)
  • Data sold to third parties: No
  • Users can request deletion: Yes — see the Delete Account section above
  • Data shared with government officials: Only for grievance resolution, scoped to your constituency
  • AI processing: Only text/images you explicitly submit to AI features are sent to Google Gemini. Aadhaar numbers, passwords, and auth tokens are never sent to any AI service.

Permissions & Prominent Disclosure

  • Camera: Rationale dialog shown before the OS prompt
  • Location: Rationale dialog shown before the OS prompt
  • Contacts: In-app disclosure shown before the OS prompt; only selected contacts are processed
  • Microphone: Rationale dialog shown before the OS prompt
  • Notifications: Requested after sign-in with user-visible explanation
